MyFedPlan Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026

MyFedPlan respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have regarding your information when you use https://www.myfedplan.us.

Information We Collect

  • Contact information you voluntarily provide (such as email address).
  • Technical data such as browser type, device type, pages visited, and approximate location.
  • Cookies and similar technologies used for analytics, preferences, and advertising support.

How We Use Information

  • To operate and improve calculators and site features.
  • To respond to support requests and account-related questions.
  • To analyze usage trends and improve performance.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

Advertising and Third Parties

We may use third-party services including analytics and advertising providers. These services may use cookies and similar technologies to understand site usage and personalize advertising. We do not sell personal information.

Your Choices

  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
  • You may contact us to request updates or deletion of personal information we hold, where applicable.
  • You can opt out of marketing communications by using unsubscribe links or contacting us directly.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Contact

If you have privacy questions, contact us at support@myfedplan.com.
